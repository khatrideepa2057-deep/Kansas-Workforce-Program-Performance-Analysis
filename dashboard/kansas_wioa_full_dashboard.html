ERQ2 represents employment in the second quarter after program exit. ERQ4 represents employment in the fourth quarter after exit. MEQ2 represents median earnings during the second quarter after exit.

Performance results are compared with Kansas statewide program benchmarks. Suppressed or invalid values are treated as unavailable rather than zero. Rates with invalid numerator or denominator values were excluded from benchmark comparisons.
